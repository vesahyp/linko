# Linko

A shooter round a planet whose pull bends every ship and every bullet.
Phones first, in landscape. **Play: https://vesahyp.github.io/linko/**

## How to play

- **Left thumb** flies the ship: drag where you want to go. Let go and the
  ship falls free round the planet. The dotted line ahead of the ship is
  where that fall takes you.
- **Right thumb** charges the wave cannon while it is down. Drag to aim
  (the dashed line is the shot's curved path), lift to fire. A lift with no
  drag fires at whatever the auto-aim can reach. Deep in the planet's well
  (inside the blue dotted ring) the cannon charges almost twice as fast.
- **The guns fire by themselves**, aimed with the pull in the sum.
- **POD** launches the pod. It falls into orbit like everything else,
  blocks shots, rams and fires. Tap again to call it home. Docked on the
  nose it is a shield.
- **The red ring** is the line of no return: below it the planet pulls
  harder than your engine pushes. Hitting the planet hurts. Enemies that
  hit it die and score double.
- **Slingshot:** dive through the well and come out fast for a half charge.
- Between waves pick one of three upgrades for the ship or the guns. Each
  one shows on the ship.

Keyboard: WASD to fly, hold the mouse button to charge and aim, release to
fire, space for the pod.
