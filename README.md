# Linko

An arcade shooter in the shape of R-Type, phones first, in landscape: a
stage that scrolls, formations to break, bullets to read and dodge, a charge
beam, a pod, and bosses with phases and weak points.
**Play: https://vesahyp.github.io/linko/**

## How to play

- **Left thumb: drag to fly.** Put your thumb down anywhere on the left
  two thirds of the screen and move it; the ship moves with it, a little
  further than your thumb. Lift and put it down again to go further.
- **The guns fire by themselves**, forward.
- **Right thumb: hold to charge the beam, lift to fire.** The BEAM bar at
  the bottom fills; a full beam goes through a whole line of enemies.
- **POD:** launches the pod ahead of you. It blocks shots, rams, and fires
  your gun. Tap again (RECALL) to call it home. It docks on the side it
  comes back to: behind you it guards your back and fires backward.
- **Power-ups:** shoot the glass carriers, and shoot down every ship of a
  formation. Crystals set your gun: red is a spread, blue a laser that
  goes through, yellow homing shots. The same colour again makes it
  stronger. S is speed, M missiles, B a bit drone (two at most), + repair.
  Everything you pick up shows on the ship.
- **Bosses:** armour turns your shots away. Shoot the guns off, and hit the
  glowing core when it opens. A red dashed line is a beam about to fire:
  get off it.
- After each boss, pick one lasting upgrade for the ship. Three stages,
  then round two, harder.
- Lost your ship? **Continue** starts the stage again with your ship as it
  was and the score at zero.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to fly, hold space to charge, E for the pod.
